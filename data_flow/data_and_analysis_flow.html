How to use

  • Scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan.
  • Drag a node to move it.
  • Shift-drag (or Ctrl-drag) on the background to select a box of nodes, then Zoom to selection.
  • Click a node to select it; Shift-click or Ctrl-click to add more.
  • Lineage of selection highlights what the selection depends on and what depends on it; Hide the rest then leaves only that and lays it out compactly.
  • Re-layout shown lays out the nodes now shown, for example after unchecking some kinds. Reset restores the full graph and its original layout.
