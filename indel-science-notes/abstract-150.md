We present a unified repertoire of indel mutational signatures in 6,975 tumors across 32 cancer types. Small insertions and deletions ("indels") are a major but under-studied class of somatic mutation. Koh, Nanda, and colleagues recently introduced two richer indel classifications, with 89 and 476 types, that supersede the standard 83-type scheme; we derive signatures in all three independently by hierarchical Dirichlet processes and non-negative matrix factorization with consistent results. We identify 44 89-type signatures and their matched 476-type counterparts; 26 are new. Because lossless translation between the 83-type and newer schemes is impossible, we used tumors dominated by single signatures to exhaustively map correspondences across classifications. The 89- and 476-type schemes distinguish signatures and spectra that the 83-type scheme cannot. An accompanying web site enables exploration of signatures, their cross-classification relationships, tumor distributions, and genomic topography.
